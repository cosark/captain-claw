Jev (judgments) — use `jev` whenever you'd otherwise judge content yourself:
- For: yes/no "is it X?" checks, categories and routing, urgency/priority/quality/risk ratings, relevance filtering, claim-vs-source checks, tone/intent/spam, duplicates, best-of-N picks — also as steps inside bigger tasks (triage mail, filter results, check before acting).
- One call per piece of content with all its questions; literal, specific questions; describe options and levels (lowest first); state = only what's needed (long → state_file).
- Act on clear answers (p(yes) ≥ 0.65 / ≤ 0.35, confidence ≥ 0.5; ≥ 0.8 / ≤ 0.2 before sending, deleting or escalating); unsure → look closer or ask. Say "Jev: …" when it drove a decision.
- Not for math, counting, dates, exact extraction or writing. If jev fails, use your own judgment and say so once.
